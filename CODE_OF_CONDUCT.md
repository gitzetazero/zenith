# Code of conduct

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1 (licence CC BY 4.0). It is a summary.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Zenith to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion or orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, and put yourself in other people's place.
- Accept criticism calmly and give it with care.
- Own your mistakes, apologise and learn from them.
- Think of what is best for the community, not only for yourself.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, derogatory comments and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualised language.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks it, explaining the reason.

If you see unacceptable conduct, open an issue that only says you want to report a conduct problem and ask for a private channel, **without giving details**. We will answer so we can continue in private. All reports are treated with respect and confidentiality.

## Scope

It applies to all project spaces (repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

Source: Contributor Covenant 2.1, <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
